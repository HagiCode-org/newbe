# 隐私声明

#### 中文

我们尊重并保护您的隐私。在您使用我们的服务时，我们可能会收集并使用您的个人信息。本隐私声明旨在向您解释我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护这些信息。

##### 收集的信息

我们可能会收集您提供给我们的个人信息，如您的姓名、电子邮件地址等。我们还可能会自动收集有关您使用我们服务的信息，如访问记录、IP地址等。

##### 使用信息

我们可能会将您的个人信息用于以下用途：
- 提供、维护和改进我们的服务。
- 向您发送与服务相关的通知和信息。
- 与您沟通并解决您的问题。

##### 信息保护

我们会采取合理的安全措施来保护您的个人信息，防止未经授权的访问、使用或泄露。

##### 信息分享

我们不会未经您的明确同意向第三方分享您的个人信息，除非法律要求或允许。

##### 隐私权政策的更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私声明。更新后的隐私声明将在我们的网站上发布，并生效。

#### English

We respect and protect your privacy. When you use our services, we may collect and use your personal information. This privacy statement is intended to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect this information.

##### Information Collected

We may collect personal information that you provide to us, such as your name, email address, etc. We may also automatically collect information about your use of our services, such as access records, IP addresses, etc.

##### Use of Information

We may use your personal information for the following purposes:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
- Send you notifications and information related to the service.
- Communicate with you and address your inquiries.

##### Information Protection

We will take reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

##### Information Sharing

We will not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, unless required or allowed by law.

##### Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy statement from time to time. The updated privacy statement will be posted on our website and will take effect.
